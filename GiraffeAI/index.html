Capabilities

Interact by typing or simply by speaking, as if you were talking to a colleague — and get answers with the same naturalness.

An agent that acts

A memory that never forgets

Contracts, emails, deadlines, production reports — constantly absorbed and kept present in its digital mind, like an up-to-the-minute photograph of corporate life.

Never invents anything

Every statement, number and piece of advice comes with a precise reference. You can always verify, check and trust what it tells you.

Speed without compromise

How it works

How does it manage to be so intelligent?

No magic — just very advanced technologies working together harmoniously.

01

Decomposes the problem

When assigned a complex task, it breaks it down into many small steps, exactly like a good project manager would do.

02

Retrieves from authoritative sources

For each step it gathers the necessary information from the most reliable places: internal documents, institutional websites, public databases.

03

Delivers with references

Every statement is accompanied by a precise reference, so you can always verify where each fact comes from. No more invented answers.

Agent in action

It does not just think — it does things

The operational agent moves autonomously in the digital world, following very specific procedures that have been taught to it. And you can always check what it is doing, or simply tell it to “stop.”

  • Browses the internet and visits the right websites
  • Downloads documents and reads articles
  • Accesses portals, fills forms, replies to emails
  • Delivers complete, well-written work — ready for review

Security & control

Your data. Your rules. Total transparency.

Entrusting important data to an automatic system is a serious decision. That is why GiraffeAI was designed with security as the primary concern.

  • Data always under your controlProtected with the most advanced standards, physically installed inside your company — no outsider can access the information.
  • Every action loggedA detailed log records what it did and why — you can always go back and see the basis of every choice.
